Scoring

Clear coral & blobs — line up 4+ of one color. Every piece cleared scores points; pollution blobs are worth the most.

Chain blooms — cascading clears stack a multiplier (BLOOM ×2, ×3 …), so one well-placed drop can pay off big.

Soft drop — nudging a fragment down with ↓ adds a small bonus per cell.

Clear the depth — remove every pollution blob to restore the reef and bank a level-clear bonus.

Clean reef bonus — the fewer coral fragments left on the board when you clear it, the bigger the level-clear bonus.

Difficulty multiplier — starting at a harder depth multiplies all the points you earn (×1 at 1, up to ×2.35 at 10).
