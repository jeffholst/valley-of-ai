      How to Play Alibi

      Alibi is a 5–10 minute team-builder icebreaker. One player at a time has to invent a ridiculous-but-convincing alibi for a silly crime while everyone else decides how believable it sounded.

      Setup

      • Add 2–12 player names. Names are saved locally and reload next time.
      • Pick a round count, alibi timer, and whether the accused rotates in order or randomly.

      Each round

      1. A silly crime is shown on screen.
      2. Spin to pick The Accused.
      3. Start the timer — the accused has 30–60 seconds to improvise an alibi.
      4. Optionally open the Moderator Window on a second screen to peek at the "real truth" and prompt ideas.
      5. Reveal awards and everyone — including the accused — rates the alibi: 1 = not believable, 2 = somewhat believable, 3 = totally believable.
      6. The accused earns the sum of those ratings.

      Winning

      After the final round, the player with the highest total score wins. Ties share the same medal. There is no penalty for a bad alibi — only reward for creative, convincing ones.

      Tips for hosts

      • Keep rounds fast — don't over-explain rules between rounds.
      • Encourage hecklers to interject questions; it makes alibis funnier.
      • If the group is shy, use "Solo Practice" mode to warm up.
