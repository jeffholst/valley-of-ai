🎭 Charades Chaos Deck

How many rounds?

Each team gets 60 seconds per round.

How to Play

Objective

Get your team to guess as many words as possible before time runs out!

Setup

  • Choose 1, 2, or 3 rounds at the start of each game.
  • Each team plays a 60-second turn per round.
  • Teams alternate — Team A always goes first each round.

On your turn

  • Press ▶ Start to begin the 60-second countdown.
  • The actor sees the Target Word — act it out or describe it.
  • Taboo words may not be said, mimed, or spelled out.
  • Press 🚨 Buzzer if the opposing team thinks a taboo word was used.
  • Press Score +1 when your team guesses correctly — the next card appears.
  • Press ← Pass to skip the current card.
  • You can press ⏸ Pause to pause the timer mid-turn.

End of turn

  • When the 60-second timer reaches zero, the turn ends automatically.
  • Teams switch and the next team presses Start to begin their turn.

Winning

  • After all rounds are complete, the team with the most points wins!

Keyboard shortcuts

  • ← — pass current card
  • → — score a point
  • Space — start / pause timer
