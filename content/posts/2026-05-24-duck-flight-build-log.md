# Duck Flight: Build Log

**App:** Duck Flight
**Built:** May 24, 2026
**Model:** Claude Sonnet 4.6
**Agent:** Claude Code
**Total improvements:** 10 runs across 2 days

## The idea

The original suggestion asked for a mobile-first, first-person flying experience through-the-eyes-of-a-duck. Rings spawn far away and approach the camera. The player steers left and right to thread the centers. A momentum system starts at 1.0, drains passively, drops on a miss, rises on a good pass — hit zero and it's game over.

The prompt was specific about the first-person duck silhouette at the bottom of the screen, the 3D perspective projection with fog, and leaderboard integration.

## The scaffold build

The initial scaffold took one run and established the full architecture:

- **FOCAL_LEN=300**, viewport Y at 44% height
- Rings approach from **SPAWN_Z=3000** at 900 units/sec base, scaling with momentum
- Duck steers laterally in world range -1.6 to +1.6
- Momentum system wired: +0.12 on pass, -0.22 on miss, -0.012 passive drain
- Particle system, audio stub, animation stub all scaffolded
- Difficulty: speed +90 and interval -0.15 every 30 seconds

Not playable yet, but all the pieces were in place.

## Visual improvements (runs 2–5)

The next four improvement runs transformed the look:

**Projection refinement** — FOCAL_LEN jumped from 300 to 2000. The horizon moved to true vertical center. A three-band animated cloud sky replaced the flat background. The ground got a proper perspective grid. Fog color blending was added (clear at z=40, fogged at z=120). DPR scaling for retina screens.

**Duck POV** — Three-layer bezier wings were built: primary, secondary, and covert feathers with per-layer gradients. An off-yellow beak hint at the bottom edge. Idle bob, flap, and bank animations. Wing-tip motion blur on hard banking. Flap rate scales with momentum.

**Shape variety** — The fixed ring-only format was replaced with three shape types: ring, triangle, and square. Each carries its own size, inner radius, HSL hue, spin axis (face-on twirl or barrel-roll), rotation speed, and rotation. Rendering uses projected polygon vertices with a top-light gradient fill and specular highlight.

**Gameplay feel** — Forward speed was tied directly to momentum. Difficulty now ramps every 10 passes rather than time-based. A STALLING state with 0.75-second slow-motion and screen darkening precedes the game over overlay.

## Visual polish (runs 6–7)

The golden-hour visual pass replaced the sky with a deep teal-to-peach-gold palette cycling over 60 seconds. A sun disc with radial bloom and lens-flare streaks appears when steering toward it. Ground updated to warm sand-to-umber. Edge vignette added.

The juice pass upgraded the pass-through burst to 28 glowing, stretching particles. Near-miss sparkle trails at 200ms. Speed lines radiating from VP at high momentum. Pulsing red stall-warning vignette below 30% momentum. Floating +10 score popups on each clear.

## UI overhaul (run 8)

The DOM overlay was removed and everything moved onto the canvas. The MENU state shows the animated world background with a hovering duck silhouette, gold-glow title, and best score. HUD redesigned to large outlined score numerals with a centered momentum bar. GAMEOVER state renders the dimmed world with final score, best score, and a pulsing retry prompt. Best score persisted to localStorage.

## Mobile polish and physics tuning (runs 9–10)

DPR capped at 2. Touch events wired with `passive: false`. Haptics via `navigator.vibrate` (10ms on pass, 60ms on game over). Inertia-based lateral movement with proportional drag. Amplified banking with slower spring. World geometry rolling during banks. Ground grid scrolling added.

The pacing was then deliberately slowed: base speed reduced, shapes spawn closer and less frequently, fog starts earlier, PASS_Z moved from 60 to 28 world units (shapes fill the viewport at check time for a genuine fly-through feel). The speed multiplier from momentum was removed so shapes approach at constant speed.

## The flappy bird mechanic

The final improvement added vertical flight. The duck now has gravity (3.5 units/sec²) and each tap applies an upward impulse. Altitude replaced passive momentum drain as the primary survival mechanic — hit the ground and the duck stalls. The pass check uses duck altitude for vertical ring alignment. An altitude bar was added on the right canvas edge.

The initial tap impulse of 3.8 proved too aggressive, causing players to overshoot rings. A subsequent fix reduced this to 2.4 and changed the impulse from a hard velocity overwrite to an additive-capped formula — making taps proportional to the current state rather than always snapping to the same velocity.

## What worked well

The model handled the 3D perspective math correctly from the first run. The FOCAL_LEN and viewport coordinate system were consistent across all subsequent improvements. Wing bezier construction and banking animation were both produced without manual correction.

## What needed human guidance

Pacing required multiple human-directed adjustments. The initial difficulty curve was too steep (speed scaling too aggressive, PASS_Z too early). The flappy-bird mechanic was suggested by the human and required two improvement runs to get the feel right — the additive impulse fix in particular was a human diagnosis of why the tapping felt wrong.

The three-zone touch control scheme (left/center/right for steer/flap/steer) was also human-directed after discovering that tap-to-flap simultaneously steered the duck.
